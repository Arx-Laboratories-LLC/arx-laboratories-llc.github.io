TCGBox — Privacy Policy

Last updated 13 October 2026

TCGBox is a Magic: The Gathering collection and deck-building app. It has no servers and no user accounts. Your collection lives in a database on your own device. There is nowhere for us to see it, because there is no "us" to send it to.

This policy describes the few cases where data does leave your device, and exactly what is sent in each one.

What stays on your device

  • Your collection, decks and boxes. Stored locally.
  • Card photos. Scanning uses the camera, but recognition runs entirely on your device (Google ML Kit, on-device models). Images are never uploaded or stored by us.
  • Crash details. If the app crashes it writes a file on your device so you can read what happened. It is never transmitted.
  • Settings and preferences.

What leaves your device, and when

Goes toWhat is sentWhen
Google AdMob Your device's advertising identifier and standard device/network information, used to show ads. On every launch, unless you have bought the ad-free upgrade. In the UK and EEA you are asked for consent first.
Commander Spellbook The card names in the deck you are analysing. Nothing else — no account, no device identifier, no collection. Only if you switch on "Check decks against Commander Spellbook". Off by default.
Commander Spellbook (second, separate option) Additionally, the names of cards you own that match the deck's colours, so it can say which combos you could assemble. Only if you also switch on "Also suggest combos you could build". Off by default, and it does nothing unless the option above is on too.
Google Drive A backup of your own collection data, written to your Drive's private app folder. It is your copy, in your Drive. We cannot read it. Only if you sign in and enable backup.
UPCitemdb A barcode you scanned from a sealed product, to identify the product. Only when you scan a pack barcode that is not in the app's offline table.
Scryfall, EDHREC, Frankfurter, Wizards of the Coast Nothing about you. The app downloads card data, prices, exchange rates and news from these services. As with any web request, they can see your IP address. When updating the card database or showing news.
Google Play Purchases are handled by Google Play Billing. We never see your payment details. If you buy the ad-free upgrade.

What we do not do

  • No analytics or usage tracking of any kind.
  • No crash-reporting service. Crash files stay on your device.
  • No accounts, no profiles, no email lists.
  • We do not sell or share your data, because we do not receive it.

Advertising

Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which may use your advertising identifier to personalise them. You can reset or delete that identifier in Android's Settings → Privacy → Ads. In the UK and EEA, the app asks for your consent choices through Google's consent tool before requesting ads, and you can change them later from Settings → Ad privacy options inside the app. Buying the ad-free upgrade stops ad requests entirely.

Google's own policy covers what they do with that data: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Children

TCGBox is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices

  • Everything optional is off by default. The Commander Spellbook lookups and Drive backup all require you to switch them on.
  • Turning an option off stops it immediately — the setting is checked before every request, not only when you change it.
  • Deleting the app removes the local database. If you used Drive backup, delete the backup from your Google account's Data & privacy → Apps connected to your account.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Material changes to what the app sends will also be described in the app's release notes.
